# The Smell of Coffee at 7 AM ☕

There’s something magical about the **first sip** of coffee in the morning.  
Today I decided to try a _V60 pour-over_ instead of my usual espresso.

---

## Brew Details

- **Beans**: Ethiopian Yirgacheffe, light roast
- **Grind Size**: Medium-fine
- **Water Temp**: 94°C
- **Ratio**: 1:16 (20g coffee, 320g water)

---

### Process Notes

1. Rinsed the filter to remove paper taste.
2. Bloomed for 30 seconds with 40g water.
3. Poured in slow spirals over ~2 minutes.
4. Swirled the brewer before final drip.

---

> “Life begins after coffee.”

---

### Taste Test

| Attribute | Rating (1–5) | Notes           |
| --------- | ------------ | --------------- |
| Aroma     | 5            | Floral, citrusy |
| Acidity   | 4            | Bright lemon    |
| Sweetness | 5            | Honey-like      |
| Body      | 3            | Light, tea-like |

---

### Final Thoughts

- Loved the clarity of flavor.
- Might try a slightly finer grind tomorrow.
- This could replace my espresso habit on slow mornings.
